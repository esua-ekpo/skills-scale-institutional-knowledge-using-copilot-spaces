# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide the technical direction of a project, ensure engineering quality, and coordinate architectural decisions across the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical design and architecture alignment with product goals
- Help break down complex work into manageable technical increments
- Review technical trade-offs, risks, and dependencies with the team
- Support developers in solving blocking issues and technical constraints
- Ensure work meets quality, maintainability, and delivery standards

### Goals
- Maintain a clear technical path to successful delivery
- Reduce rework caused by poor design or unclear implementation decisions
- Improve engineering consistency across the project

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Daily engineering coordination and dependency tracking
- Escalation of blockers to PM, stakeholders, or partner teams when needed

### Key Interactions
- Collaborates with Developers on implementation and quality
- Works closely with Product Managers and Project Managers on scope, risks, and sequencing
- Aligns with Security/Compliance and QA partners on release readiness and technical controls

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads define the validation strategy for the project and ensure that features meet the agreed acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain the overall test strategy and quality plan
- Define acceptance criteria validation approaches and regression coverage
- Coordinate test execution, defect triage, and release readiness checks
- Partner with developers and product owners to resolve quality gaps
- Support stakeholder confidence through evidence-based validation

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce release risk
- Ensure delivery quality is measurable and visible
- Align testing with user impact and acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- Test planning, defect review, and release sign-off discussions
- Participation in sprint demos and validation reviews
- Risk and release communication with PMs and stakeholders

### Key Interactions
- Works with Developers to validate correctness and prevent regressions
- Coordinates with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and user outcome validation
- Contributes to release decisions with Technical Leads and Project Managers

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners assess project risk, ensure appropriate controls, and help teams meet regulatory, privacy, and operational requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Review the project for security, privacy, and compliance risks
- Define required controls, review gates, and escalation paths
- Support secure architecture patterns and incident readiness
- Validate that delivery work adheres to policy, legal, and customer requirements
- Help identify and mitigate high-risk dependencies or exposures

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Enable safe and trustworthy delivery without unnecessary delay
- Provide clear guidance for risk mitigation and exception handling

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and risk assessments
- Compliance and privacy guidance during planning and release phases
- Escalation of critical findings to PM, Sponsor, or Security on-call as needed

### Key Interactions
- Provides guidance to Technical Leads and Developers on secure implementation
- Works with Project Managers and Product Managers on risk management and release gating
- Supports stakeholder confidence in governance, privacy, and operational safety

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Stakeholder Liaisons translate customer needs, stakeholder priorities, and operational concerns into delivery actions and clear communication.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback and communicate needs to product and delivery teams
- Ensure stakeholder expectations are visible and aligned with project outcomes
- Support adoption, training, and communication planning for releases
- Help prioritize issues that affect customer experience or stakeholder confidence
- Provide status updates and clarify decision impacts across groups

### Goals
- Improve customer value and satisfaction
- Maintain strong stakeholder trust and alignment
- Ensure delivery decisions reflect real-world user impact

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates, customer feedback loops, and launch communications
- Weekly or milestone-based communication on outcomes and risks
- Escalation of customer-impacting issues to product and project leadership

### Key Interactions
- Connects Product Managers and stakeholders to delivery priorities
- Collaborates with Project Managers on communications and milestone plans
- Shares customer insight back to Developers, QA, and Technical Leads for prioritization

---

## Role Accountability and Cross-Functional Coordination

Each project should make ownership explicit for key decisions and workstreams. This helps reduce ambiguity, prevents handoff gaps, and ensures stakeholders know who is accountable for outcomes.

- Product Manager: defines value, success metrics, and prioritization
- Project Manager: coordinates schedule, risks, communication, and dependencies
- Technical Lead: owns technical direction, design quality, and implementation feasibility
- QA / Test Lead: validates quality, defects, and release readiness
- Security / Compliance Partners: review risk, controls, and policy requirements
- Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison: keeps external expectations aligned with delivery reality

This model ensures the team can move quickly without losing clarity on who owns what.
